About Me

I turn product ideas into working AI-powered applications.

I am Amdebirhan Bishu, a Chicago-based AI Product Engineer and Full Stack Developer building mobile-first products with React Native, Node.js, Supabase, PostgreSQL, cloud deployment, and OpenAI workflows.

Current Focus

Building and launching DoseBridge, StyleMirror AI, and SurfIntel while pursuing software engineering opportunities in AI, mobile, and full-stack product development.

I am especially interested in products where AI makes a real workflow clearer, faster, safer, or more personal.

Product-builder Mindset

I like building the whole product loop: user flows, mobile screens, backend APIs, database structure, authentication, AI integration, deployment, testing, and iteration from real feedback.

My focus goes beyond writing code. I care about whether the product is understandable, useful, and ready for someone outside the development process to use without explanation.

My current flagship project, DoseBridge, combines medication reminders, caregiver workflows, meal and grocery support, voice notifications, GPT-backed insights, and TestFlight beta testing into one healthcare support product.

StyleMirror AI is my visual AI case study, combining AI image generation, mobile-first experiences, personalized recommendations, cultural hairstyle collections, social voting, favorites, and subscription-ready monetization into a single consumer platform.
