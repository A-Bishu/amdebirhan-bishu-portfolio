How I approach engineering

A useful product is more than a polished screen. The API, data model, recovery behavior, deployment, and testing all shape the user’s experience.

I work across that complete loop: clarifying the user problem, designing mobile and web flows, building REST APIs, modeling relational data, integrating third-party and AI services, deploying backend systems, and improving the product through testing and feedback.

I pay special attention to high-risk paths such as authentication, permissions, long-running jobs, purchases, caching, state synchronization, and result recovery.
